# Private account review copies — 19 October 2026

Signed-in account users can explicitly save a completed local review to their account. The review includes trade records used in that review, the confirmed rules, generated findings and next focus. This is optional product storage, separate from coded research activity. Original CSV files and unsaved drafts are not uploaded. The user sees a disclosure and chooses Save to account; nothing is automatically migrated from previous local sessions.

Cloudflare AUTH_DB holds account_reviews keyed by user ID and review ID, with updated_at and disclosure_version. Payload uses AES-256-GCM, a new 96-bit random nonce per write, and authenticated user/review IDs as additional data. REVIEW_ENCRYPTION_KEY is a separate server secret, retained privately. Operators can decrypt through the app; this is encryption in storage, not end-to-end encryption. Preserve the encryption key during deployments and plan a versioned migration before rotating it. Losing it makes stored copies unreadable.

All reads and writes require the signed-in account cookie. Account-backed study recording and AI tokens also require their owner’s current sign-in, so an old bearer token alone cannot survive account logout/reset. Mutations require exact same-origin JSON; bounded requests, validation, per-account request limits and a 50-review account cap apply. Findings are recomputed from validated inputs on save and restore. Account copies can be opened through History in another signed-in browser or device. New saves deliberately replace this review's previous account copy; simultaneous saves use last-write-wins, not automatic merging. Remove from account deletes the product copy; browser copies and coded study records are separate. Provider backups and retention still need final disclosure.

Completion pages offer backup/export before leaving clears session-local work. Study exports and researcher name lookup do not contain vault payloads or instruments. The researcher desk now opens a session's task completion, app-visible time, help/evidence fields and questionnaire/comment/event counts. These are not proof of trader improvement, independent task observation or actual attention.
